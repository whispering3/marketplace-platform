# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus discussion on ideas, evidence and the project.
- Give and accept feedback in good faith.
- Respect privacy and do not share confidential, personal or security-sensitive information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information, or encouraging unlawful activity are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the repository owner through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as privately as practical. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation or take other appropriate action.
